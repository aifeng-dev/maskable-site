常见问题

我的图片会被上传吗？

不会。识别、打码、标注、保存都在你的设备上完成，maskable 没有任何接收图片的服务器。

支持哪些设备？

iPhone 与 iPad，需要 iOS / iPadOS 16 或更高版本。

会修改我的原图吗？

不会覆盖原图。你可以另存为新照片，也可以更新当前照片 —— 后者通过系统相册的非破坏式编辑保存，随时能还原。

收费吗？

可免费下载使用。Pro 解锁全部功能，提供月度订阅、年度订阅和终身买断三种方式，价格以 App 内显示为准。

Questions

Are my images uploaded?

No. Detection, redaction, annotation and saving all happen on your device. maskable has no server that receives images.

Which devices are supported?

iPhone and iPad running iOS / iPadOS 16 or later.

Does it change my original photo?

It never overwrites it. Save as a new photo, or update the current one — that uses the Photos app’s non-destructive editing, so you can always revert.

How much does it cost?

Free to download and use. Pro unlocks everything and comes as a monthly subscription, a yearly subscription, or a one-time lifetime purchase; prices are shown in the app.
